02 / 07 · One app, four ways

Matched to the member, not the other way round.

  • ১২৩ a spoken, numbered menu for people who can't read
  • নতুন guided, one thing at a time
  • মাঝারি the standard screens, each button with a 🔊
  • দক্ষ everything at once, for experienced users

In the guided tiers the first tap on any button says what it does and the second tap does it, so nothing happens by accident.

03 / 07 · Adaptive

It notices how tasks go, then makes an offer.

Each everyday task is scored as clean or rough. Tapping ? twice, abandoning a task, three keypad corrections or replaying the same audio all count as rough.

  • 8 clean of the last 10, over 3 different days: offer the tier above
  • 6 rough: offer the tier below
  • The member decides. Declining pauses the offers

There's deliberately no timer. In the field, a slow task usually means an interruption, not a lack of skill.

04 / 07 · Money you can see

Amounts drawn as the notes that make them up.

Someone who can't read ৳২৮,৫০০ can still recognise twenty-eight purple ১০০০ notes and one green ৫০০. The picture updates live, even while the member is typing an amount.

05 / 07 · Trust

"Did BRAC actually get my money?"

Members often hand repayments to a field worker. Now they can check, without reading, that BRAC recorded it.

  • Every account leads with the latest payment: green when BRAC received it, red for a month with nothing
  • Every history row says how it was paid: cash, bKash or at the branch
  • "Paid, but it's not here?" calls BRAC's own call centre, not the field worker
06 / 07 · Safe by default

Mistakes are hard to make.

  • Icons from village life: bangles and taka for a loan, a clay bank for savings

01 / 06 · Interface

One button. Talk, don't type.

Press কথা বলুন ("speak") and ask. The orb shows what the assistant is doing, so the member can follow along without reading: listening, searching the documents, or speaking.

Typed questions work too, and the conversation can be shown as a transcript.

02 / 06 · Architecture

Streaming in both directions.

The browser captures the microphone at 16 kHz with an AudioWorklet and streams it over a WebSocket to a FastAPI server, which relays it to the Gemini Live API. The spoken reply streams back the same way and plays as it arrives.

The assistant starts speaking without waiting for a whole answer to be written first.

03 / 06 · Grounding

It has to look things up first.

Gemini gets exactly one tool, search_knowledge_base, and instructions that begin:

"You have no knowledge of your own. Every factual statement you make must come from a passage returned by the search tool."
04 / 06 · Retrieval

Hybrid search, tuned for Bengali.

Documents are split by heading into passages of about 900 characters, and every question is scored two ways:

  • Meaning: Gemini embeddings find passages that say the same thing in different words
  • Words: word and character-trigram matching catch exact names. Trigrams cope with Bengali endings: ঋণ, ঋণের, ঋণগুলো

Embeddings are cached by content, so a restart doesn't re-embed unchanged documents.

05 / 06 · Honesty

When it doesn't know, it says so.

If nothing relevant comes back, it doesn't guess. If a question is only partly covered, it answers that part and says which part it can't. Greetings and small talk skip the search entirely.

06 / 06 · No code needed · Live

Admins run it from a panel. You can talk to it now.

  • Choose from 8 Gemini voices and tune how strict the search is
  • Upload a PDF, photo or Word file: Gemini rewrites it as clean text and the knowledge base re-indexes itself
  • Change the API key without touching the server

01 / 05 · Why local

Private, free to run, independent of the internet.

Member questions and BRAC documents never leave the machine, there's no per-use API bill, and it keeps working where connectivity is poor. The trade-off: we carry the hardware and the quality of open models.

02 / 05 · Pipeline

Nine stages, one machine.

A detector notices when you stop talking. Your speech is transcribed, matched against the documents, answered by a local language model and spoken back phrase by phrase.

Every model is pre-trained and openly licensed. The engineering is the integration: real-time turn-taking, interruption, Bengali text handling, retrieval and instrumentation.

03 / 05 · Speed

It starts speaking before the answer is finished.

The model streams its answer word by word. As soon as the first phrase is complete it goes to speech synthesis. While phrase one plays, phrase two is being synthesised and the model is still writing phrase three.

Heavy GPU work runs off the main loop, so listening never stalls.

04 / 05 · Conversation

A conversation, not a command line.

  • Interruptions: talk over it and it stops at once, drops what it was about to say, and listens
  • Fillers: if no answer has started within 700 ms, a short pre-recorded phrase plays
  • Spoken style: no lists, symbols or emoji, because everything is read aloud
  • Memory: the last six messages go with each question
05 / 05 · Bengali text

Numbers read the way a person says them.

Speech models stumble over digits and symbols. Before synthesis, amounts, percentages, dates and decimals, in Bengali or Latin digits, are rewritten as Bengali words.

Every visit, on a map of Bangladesh.

From the whole country to one branch.

01 / 04 · The problem

A missed instalment is the start of a story.

When a member misses a scheduled instalment, the field officer records why and when they'll pay. The app then models the whole cycle: grace period, collection, partial payment, re-miss and escalation.
